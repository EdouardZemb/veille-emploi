# PROVISIONAL FOLLOW-UP TEMPLATE

A skeleton, not a letter. The console fills the markers with recorded facts;
it writes no sentence for you. Drop your own `relance_en.md` in your kit and
yours will be used instead.

Markers: `{{employeur}}` `{{titre}}` `{{lieu}}` `{{contact}}`
`{{contact_civilite}}` `{{date_candidature}}` `{{jours}}` `{{canal}}` `{{date}}`.
